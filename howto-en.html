1Goal

A cat curled up into a ball rolls from the front door deeper and deeper into the house.

Dodge furniture and animals, smash sparkling things ✨ in a row for combo points, and see how many rooms you can reach!

The cat rolls forward on its own. The farther you go, the faster it gets — and the music speeds up too.

3The cat's two forms

🟠 Ball (on the floor)

Most of the time the cat is curled up into a ball, rolling along the floor.

Only the ball can roll through a cat flap.

🐈 Cat (jumping or on furniture)

When it jumps, the cat stretches out and turns back into a cat.

Land on a table or a chair and it trots along the top.

4Things you can smash ✨

Anything that sparkles and has a soft glow around its base can be smashed — as a ball or as a cat. 20 points each, up to 100 with a combo.

On the floor

  • Potted plants and flower pots
  • Electric fans
  • Robot vacuums (they wander left and right)
  • Laundry (piles and baskets)
  • Vases on pedestals, block towers, lanterns, ball baskets

On tables and counters

  • Vases and bud vases
  • Goldfish bowls
  • Piggy banks
  • Small potted plants
  • Dishes, teacups, teapots, mugs, glasses, bottles, figures

To smash things on a table, jump onto the table and walk into them.

5Hit these and you're out

You only get one life. Bump into any of these and the game is over.

Furniture and fixed objects

  • Tables, low tables, kotatsu, sofas, beds, pool and table-tennis tables, the piano
  • Chairs and stools
  • Cardboard boxes, umbrella stands, vacuum cleaners, trolleys, trunks, crates
  • Lamps, braziers, folding screens, globes, music stands, speakers

Animals, moving things and walls

  • The vacuum cleaner charging down the hallway
  • The big cat in the Japanese living room
  • The big dog in the drawing room
  • The wall around each cat flap

6Climbing onto furniture

Tables, sofas, beds, chairs and similar furniture can be jumped onto. Hitting them from the side is out, but landing on top is fine.

If your jump is a little short, the cat will pull itself up as long as it reaches the edge.

Blocking furniture

Some furniture spans the whole room, so you can't go around it. Jump over it.

  • The step up from the entrance hall
  • A bed placed sideways
  • Kitchen and bar counters
  • A low bookshelf, a sideways table-tennis table, a brick planter, a wall of crates

7Cat flaps lead to the next room

Between rooms there's a wall, with a cat flap at the bottom of a door, a sliding screen, or a secret bookshelf.

  • Steer left or right yourself to line up with the flap.
  • Roll through as a ball, on the floor. If you're still in the air, you'll bonk the wall above it!
  • Miss the flap and you hit the wall — you're out.

There's nothing in your way just before a cat flap or just after it. Take your time to line up.

8Room order

Entrance Hall → Hallway → Living Room (Japanese) → Veranda → Tatami Room → Bedroom → Kids’ Room → Study → Secret Hobby Room → Kitchen → Home Bar → Karaoke Room → Billiard Room → Table Tennis Room → Brick Fireplace Room → Drawing Room → Music Room → Indoor Garden → Attic

After the attic you start again from the hallway. There are 19 rooms in all.

  • Veranda: glass doors onto the garden. Smash the bonsai and mosquito coils. Watch out for the shogi board.
  • Kids’ Room: colorful floor and bunk beds. Smash the block towers. Watch out for the rocking horse and toy box.
  • Study: bookshelves everywhere. A low bookshelf blocks the way. Watch out for piles of books.
  • Secret Hobby Room: entered through a hidden bookshelf door in the study. Smash the figures. Climb onto the model railway.
  • Home Bar: the bar counter blocks the way. Smash the glasses and bottles. Watch out for bar stools.
  • Karaoke Room: a mirror ball and a big screen. Smash the tambourines and maracas. Watch out for mic stands and speakers.
  • Billiard Room: climb onto the pool table and smash the balls on it. Watch out for the cue stands.
  • Table Tennis Room: climb onto the table (a sideways one blocks the way). Smash the ball baskets.
  • Brick Fireplace Room: brick walls and a roaring fireplace. Smash lanterns and figurines. Watch out for log piles and rocking chairs.
  • Music Room: climb onto the grand piano. Smash the metronomes. Watch out for music stands and cello cases.
  • Indoor Garden: a glass sunroom. Smash flower pots and watering cans. A brick planter blocks the way.
  • Attic: a sloping roof and beams. Smash the lanterns. Watch out for old trunks, crates and the rocking horse.

9Room surprises

Hallway: the charging vacuum

It heads straight for you and follows you left and right. Dodge it or jump over it.

Japanese living room: the sleeping big cat

Roll past it as a ball and it wakes up with a hiss, fur standing on end, and gets bigger. Jump over it quietly and it stays asleep.

Kitchen: the wet floor

Roll through spilled water and you slide sideways, out of control for a moment. Watch out for what's waiting on the other side!

Drawing room: the sleeping big dog

It sleeps along one side of the room. Bump into it and you're out. Jump over it and it barks “Woof!”

10Score

Score = distance traveled (m) + points for things smashed

Combos

Smash the next thing within 2.5 seconds to keep a combo going and raise the multiplier.

Combo1st2nd3rd4th5th and up
Points each2040 (×2)60 (×3)80 (×4)100 (×5)

During a combo, “3 COMBO! ×3” and a timer bar appear at the top of the screen. Going out of your way to smash things pays off fast.

How far you got

The 🚪 at the top left shows which room you're in. The game-over screen says something like “Reached: Tatami Room (room 5)”, and your farthest room is saved as your record (later laps show as “lap 2”).

Your best score and farthest room are saved in your browser.

The music is Offenbach’s “Galop Infernal” (the can-can) — the classic tune for races at Japanese school sports days.
